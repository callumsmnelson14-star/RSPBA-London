Pathway

From beginner to top piper.

A complete pathway for pipers and drummers in the south of England. Formal SQA-accredited qualifications, the CLASP English League for adult amateur soloists, Branch training days through the season, and the RSPBA Summer School in Glasgow each July.

01 · Qualify

SQA-accredited Piping & Drumming Qualifications

Overview

Branch members can gain formal qualifications accredited by the Scottish Qualifications Authority (SQA). The exam is delivered through the Piping & Drumming Qualifications Board (PDQB) and run annually in late March.

Register with the Branch Education Officer to be matched with a tutor; tuition is delivered online and the syllabus runs through the year up to the exam window.

Register

Register interest with the Branch Education Officer, Cliff Hall on lastminutepiper@icloud.com.

02 · Compete

CLASP — English League for Adult Amateur Soloists

About

CLASP (the Competition League for Amateur Solo Pipers) is the Branch's pathway for adult amateur solo pipers. Members compete only against same-graded players, and can move up the grading ladder from Grade 4 to Grade 1 in both piobaireachd and light music as skills improve.

CLASP also runs online practice-chanter contests for Grade 4 adult learners, creating a complete pathway from beginner to top piper.

Eligibility

CLASP is for pipers aged 18 and over. The Branch continues to offer Juvenile solo piping and drumming contests at local events for younger players.

03 · Develop

Branch Training Days

Overview

Branch training days run two or three times a season, streamed into Competition pipers, Band players and Developing/learner players, with rotating senior tutors. Drummers are taught separately by accredited snare and tenor tutors.

Recent venues have included Reading Scottish Pipe Band's practice hall and the University of Bedfordshire campus. Lunch is included; cost is typically £25 to Branch members and £45 to non-members.

04 · Excel

RSPBA Summer School

Glasgow · July

The RSPBA Summer School is held each July at RSPBA Headquarters in Washington Street, Glasgow. Aimed at pipers and drummers aged 10 and over, it's designed to support players in raising their performance to the next grade and to provide structured tutoring on rhythm, melody, harmony and reading music.

Sessions are streamed by current competition grade and led by senior tutors from across the Association.
