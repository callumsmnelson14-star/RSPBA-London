About the Branch

The London & South of England Branch of the Royal Scottish Pipe Band Association — registered charity 1183503, founded in 1965 to promote and develop pipe bands across the south of England.

Branch History

Six decades of pipe-band music in the south.

The Branch was founded in 1965 to support and grow the pipe-band tradition outside Scotland. Across six decades it has hosted indoor and outdoor contests, run training programmes for pipers and drummers, and supported member bands in competing at the RSPBA Major Championships.

The Branch is the regional home of the All-England Championships, hosted annually at the Chatsworth Country Fair in Derbyshire. Other long-running fixtures include Pipes in the Park at Colchester (since 1972), the PM Jim McGinn Memorial Contest, the Corby Highland Gathering, and the indoor Mini Band Contest at Gordon's School, Woking.

The Branch is governed by an elected Committee of officials drawn from its member bands, and operates as a Charitable Incorporated Organisation (CIO) registered with the Charity Commission.

Branch Safeguarding

Our safeguarding commitment.

The Branch is committed to safeguarding the welfare of every player, especially those under 18 and adults at risk. All Branch officials, contest stewards and adjudicators with regular contact with young people hold current DBS clearance and have completed safeguarding training appropriate to their role.

The Branch follows the RSPBA HQ Safeguarding Policy and the wider safeguarding requirements of the Charity Commission. Concerns can be raised in confidence with the Branch Safeguarding Officer.
